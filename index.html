THE LOGIC

No tricks.
Just probability.

Chance of at least one success
P = 1 − (1 − p)n

p = chance per chest · n = number of chests. Assumes independent attempts with the same active drop rate.

The important distinction

Average ≠ guaranteed. Even after 137,500 normal chests, you still have about a 36.8% chance of having found nothing.
