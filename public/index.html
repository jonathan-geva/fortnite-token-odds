How the odds work

Does the next chest get luckier?

Under the independent-roll model, every eligible chest has the same chance at a given rate. The chance of finding at least one after n chests is 1 − (1 − p)n, where p is the per-chest probability. At normal odds that’s 63.21% after 137,500 chests.

Where do the drop rates come from?

Epic lists 1 in 137,500 for Pirate Shop Access in a normal match and a 1.5× boost during Treasure Hours. Fortnite Status gave a 2× boost for a specific November 7 window as an example. Check Epic’s announcements for the multiplier in a specific session. Epic Games Support ↗ · Fortnite Status ↗

What does the simulator count?

Each run samples the wait until the first Pirate Shop Access drop. Batch rolls stop at the first success inside the batch. Changing the drop rate starts a fresh run. Other loot is outside this simulation.
